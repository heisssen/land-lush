Frequently Asked
What services do you offer?
Premium brow shaping, lamination, and bespoke lash extensions — everything designed to frame your natural beauty. Both Alena (Master Artist) and Tanya (Brow / Lash Specialist) are available to book.
How long does an appointment take?
Signature Brow Shape: 30–45 min · Brow Lamination: up to 1 hr · Lash Lift: 1 hr · Volume Full Set: up to 2.5 hrs. Extended first-visit slots include a full consultation.
Can you help me choose a style?
Absolutely. Extended new-client appointments include a consultation where we examine your bone structure, natural hair growth, and styling goals to map a fully bespoke look.
What's the lash refill policy?
Fills recommended every 2–3 weeks. 2-week fill requires ≥60% natural lashes remaining; 3-week fill requires ≥40–50%. Beyond 3 weeks = new Full Set. Arrive with clean, makeup-free lashes.
Is there a cancellation policy?
48-hour notice required to reschedule or cancel. Late cancellations incur a 50% fee; no-shows are charged 100% of the service price. We offer a 10-minute grace period for late arrivals.
Are there any contraindications?
Brow Lamination and Lash Lift are not recommended while pregnant or breastfeeding, or if you use Retin-A / Retinol. Please avoid Retin-A products 72 hours before a brow appointment. A free Patch Test is available for sensitive skin.
